Recovery workflow Safe, separate targets ↗

A backup job proves a dump was written. Restore it into an empty, separate database, compare the saved ticket manifest, then verify a transaction through the restored application. Never replace the active database to demonstrate recovery.

From the repository: python scripts/backup.py --help and python scripts/restore.py --help. The complete procedure and measured evidence are in docs/RECOVERY.md and docs/EVIDENCE_INDEX.md.

Cloud transfer, certificate trust, and external notifications require their configured dependencies. This interface performs no infrastructure or recovery actions.
