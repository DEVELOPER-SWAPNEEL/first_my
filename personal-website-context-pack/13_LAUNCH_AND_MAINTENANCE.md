# LAUNCH AND MAINTENANCE

## Launch checklist

### Identity

- name correct;
- headline correct;
- current role wording verified;
- aspirations clearly labeled;
- no exaggerated expertise;
- public location decision approved.

### Links

- GitHub;
- LinkedIn;
- email;
- resume;
- project repositories;
- demos;
- article links.

### Confidentiality

Review every project for:

- IP addresses;
- hostnames;
- credentials;
- customer data;
- company-only screenshots;
- private repository names;
- security-sensitive architecture;
- proprietary code.

### Technical

- no console errors;
- no broken routes;
- no missing images;
- no hydration warnings;
- sitemap valid;
- social cards render;
- contact works;
- resume downloads;
- custom 404;
- metadata complete.

### Motion

- no major dropped frames;
- no scroll locking bugs;
- mobile fallbacks;
- reduced-motion mode;
- tab visibility pauses continuous effects.

### Accessibility

- keyboard navigation;
- focus states;
- labels;
- alt text;
- contrast;
- heading structure;
- menu semantics.

## Maintenance cadence

### Monthly

- update current focus if materially changed;
- check broken links;
- review latest writing/project state;
- check analytics lightly;
- review dependency/security updates.

### Quarterly

- update role/profile if needed;
- replace weak featured work with stronger work;
- review resume;
- review homepage positioning;
- review search visibility;
- review performance.

### Before active job search

Increase emphasis on:

- strongest relevant projects;
- resume;
- current role targeting;
- case studies;
- recruiter clarity;
- contact path.

## Content freshness rules

Do not leave:

- “currently learning X” unchanged for years;
- old resume links;
- outdated role descriptions;
- broken demos;
- claims based on obsolete evidence.

## Analytics interpretation

Meaningful signals:

- project detail views;
- article engagement;
- resume clicks;
- contact actions;
- inbound recruiter references to specific work.

Do not over-optimize:

- raw pageviews;
- vanity counters;
- animation interaction count.

## Website quality scoreboard

Periodically evaluate:

```text
Positioning clarity
Proof quality
Project depth
Case-study clarity
Writing quality
Visual coherence
Motion quality
Performance
Accessibility
Mobile quality
SEO foundation
Content freshness
Recruiter usability
```

The goal is a useful professional asset, not a trophy page.
